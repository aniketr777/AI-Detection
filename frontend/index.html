Category: LLM Reliability | Author: Marcus Chen (Staff ML Infrastructure Engineer)

Executive Summary: Why foundation models hallucinate facts, and how hybrid dense-sparse retrieval, reranking, and citation-backed verification ensure 99.8% output factual accuracy.

Production systems deploy a four-layered grounding architecture to eliminate unverified assertions: combining vector semantic similarity with BM25 keyword matching, cross-encoder candidate reranking, strict citation prompt contracts, and post-generation fact-checking guardrails to reduce hallucination rates under 0.2%.
